About me

I grew up on a farm in Snæfellsnes, where I took an active part in my family's farming and horse breeding. The tasks on the farm were varied — among other things I cleaned Malarrif Lighthouse, filled in for the deckhand on my family's fishing boat, served fish soup to guests at Fjöruhúsið café, and drove a herd of horses across the central highlands for the National Horse Championships. I moved to Reykjavík to attend Reykjavik Junior College (MR), where I met my husband Óskar Ingi, who works as CFO of Bera. We have three children together. I graduated with a B.Sc in Industrial Engineering from the University of Iceland and a Master's degree in Management Science and Engineering from Columbia University. During my university years, my husband and I worked caring for children with disabilities at summer camps and winter stays in Reykjadalur, as well as providing personal assistance during the winter.
